# Transcription Conventions

## Book Information

- **Title:** 18.785 Number Theory I, Lecture #1
- **Author:** Andrew V. Sutherland
- **Date:** Fall 2025, 9/4/2025
- **Subject area:** Number theory (absolute values, discrete valuations, DVRs, integral extensions)

## Math Formatting

- **Inline math:** `$...$`
- **Display math:** `$$...$$`
- Block-level equations that are displayed separately from text use display math.

## Structural Elements

- **Section headers:** `## N.M Title` (matching the original numbering, e.g., `## 1.1 Introduction`)
- **Theorem/Lemma/Proposition/Corollary/Definition/Remark/Example environments:** Bold label followed by period, then statement text. E.g., `**Theorem 1.8** (Ostrowski's Theorem).` or `**Definition 1.2.**`
- **Proof:** `*Proof.*` followed by the proof text, ending with a paragraph containing `$\square$` or the text "See [citation]."
- **Bullet lists:** Use `- ` for bullet points (named properties of DVRs, etc.)
- **Numbered lists:** Use `1.` `2.` `3.` for numbered conditions/axioms

## Specific Notation

- **Absolute value:** `$|x|$`, `$|x|_p$`, `$|x|_\infty$`
- **p-adic valuation:** `$v_p$`
- **Integers mod p (finite field):** `$\mathbb{F}_p$`, `$\mathbb{F}_q$`
- **Rational numbers:** `$\mathbb{Q}$`
- **Integers:** `$\mathbb{Z}$`
- **Real numbers:** `$\mathbb{R}$`
- **Localization:** `$\mathbb{Z}_{(p)}$`
- **Fraction field:** `$\operatorname{Frac} A$`
- **Power series ring:** `$k[[t]]$`
- **Laurent series field:** `$k((t))$`
- **Polynomial ring:** `$\mathbb{F}_q[t]$`, `$A[x]$`
- **Field extensions:** `$Q(i)$`, `$\mathbb{Q}[x]/(x^2+1)$`
- **Integral closure:** `$\tilde{A}$` (with `\tilde`) or described in prose
- **Ring of integers of a number field:** `$\mathcal{O}_K$`
- **Isomorphism/congruence:** `$\simeq$`
- **Product formula:** `$\prod_{p \leq \infty} |x|_p = 1$`
- **Value group:** image of $v$ in $\mathbb{R}$
- **Uniformizer:** $\pi$
- **Maximal ideal:** $\mathfrak{m}$

## Algebraic Closure

- Algebraic closure of $K$: `$\overline{K}$`

## Integral Closure

- Integral closure notation: `$\tilde{A}$` (with `\tilde{A}`)
- Ring of integers: `$\mathcal{O}_K$`

## Colon-equals

- Use `$\colonequals$` for `:=` definitions (e.g., $v_p(0) \colonequals \infty$)

## References

References are cited as `[N]` in text and listed at end of document as numbered list with italic titles.

## Page Footer

Each page ends with a footer of the form "18.785 Fall 2025, Lecture #1, Page N" — this is omitted from transcriptions.

## Proof Ending

Proofs end with `$\square$` on the same line as the last sentence. Short proofs that cite a problem set ("See Problem Set 1.") end with `$\square$` immediately after.

## Page Continuation

Pages 4 and 5 are a continuation: page 4 ends mid-section (§1.5 DVR properties list) and page 5 begins with the final bullet and then Theorem 1.16. Each page is transcribed as-is (starting/ending where the PDF page starts/ends).
