**Corollary 1.5.** *In a field of positive characteristic every absolute value is nonarchimedean, and the only absolute value on a finite field is the trivial one.*

*Proof.* Suppose $k$ has positive characteristic $p$. Then every $n = 1 + \cdots + 1$ lies in the prime field $\mathbb{F}_p$ of $k$ and satisfies $n^p = n$. This implies $|n|^p = |n|$, so $|n|$ is either $0$ or $1$ and $k$ is nonarchimedean (by the lemma). If $k$ is finite, say of cardinality $q$, then $x^q = x$ for all $x \in k$ and $|x|^q = |x|$ is $0$ or $1$, with the former occurring only for $x = 0$. $\square$
